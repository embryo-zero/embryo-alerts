# Embryo Alerts

Embryo Alerts is Embryo-Zero's fork of [alertmanager](https://github.com/prometheus/alertmanager) (373edcb, commit 373edcb). It does sends alerts when numbers go wrong.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (COPYRIGHT.txt, LICENSE, NOTICE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
